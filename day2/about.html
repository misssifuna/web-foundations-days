About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing ideas, reminders, and tasks before they disappear. It helps you keep your thoughts organized so you can focus on what matters.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and enter your idea in the note field.
  2. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  3. Review your notes whenever you need to remember an idea.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • Keep your notes organized in one place.
  • Use keyboard shortcuts for faster note-taking.
  • Access your notes whenever you need them.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save the current note
